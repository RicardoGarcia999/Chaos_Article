\textbf{Responses to the comments of Reviewer \#4:} \\

Reviewer' comments:
\\
\\
This manuscript presents a hybrid pseudorandom number generator combining
continuous and discrete hyperchaotic systems. The design employs dynamic parametric
perturbations and modular multiplication to mitigate digital degradation. The generator is
implemented on an FPGA platform. The authors validate randomness using standard
statistical suites like NIST and TestU01.
This manuscript presents an interesting hardware design of a hybrid hyperchaotic
generator, showing promising potential for cryptographic applications. The proposed
dynamic perturbation mechanism effectively addresses digital degradation. However,
several critical technical details require substantial improvement before publication.
Specifically, the numerical discretization methodology, hardware performance evaluation
lacking operating frequency data, and statistical testing protocols need deeper theoretical
justification and more rigorous experimental validation to prove practical feasibility.
Therefore, my recommendation is Major Revision. I strongly encourage the authors to
carefully address the following detailed concerns.
\\
\\
1. The abstract should be refined to concisely address the research background, identify
the gap, state the motivation, outline the study’s content, summarize key findings, and
highlight the significance of the work. Please refer to Exploiting robust quadratic
polynomial hyperchaotic map and pixel fusion strategy for efficient image encryption and
Novel approach to time series forecasting based on memristive Hopfield Neural Network
with hidden heterogeneous and homogeneous extreme multistability for improvements.
\\
\\
\textbf{\textit{Response to this suggestion:}}
\textit{Abstract changed to better identify highlights and recommended references were added.}
\\
\\
2. I think the keyword list should be adjusted. Generally, we should select 5-7 precise
keywords that accurately represent the study’s central problem, technical approach, and
principal results, thereby optimizing indexing, discoverability, and relevance to the
journal's scope. Please refer to Lossless frequency-domain image encryption via 3D
exponential hyper-chaotic map and integer lifting wavelet transform and Extreme
multistability in discrete memristive neuron maps and implications for dual-field
applications for revisions.
\\
\\ 
\textbf{\textit{Response to this suggestion:}}
\textit{More precise keywords were used}
\\
\\
3. The introduction section lacks some essential elements. For example, the authors
should provide a brief introduction, analysis, and discussion of chaotic system
construction and its extensive applications, such as Exploiting robust quadratic
polynomial hyperchaotic map and pixel fusion strategy for efficient image encryption,
Novel approach to time series forecasting based on memristive Hopfield Neural Network
with hidden heterogeneous and homogeneous extreme multistability, Extreme
multistability in discrete memristive neuron maps and implications for dual-field
applications, and Multi-scroll hopfield neural network excited by memristive selfsynapses and its application in image encryption
\\
\\
\textbf{\textit{Response to this suggestion:}}
\textit{In section 2, discussion of modern PRNG approaches has been added}
\\
\\
4. In Section 3, using the Euler method with a tiny step size introduces correlation
between consecutive states. Please reevaluate the discretization strategy or adopt
advanced solvers to ensure the generated sequences maintain true unpredictability.
\\
\\
\textbf{\textit{Response to this suggestion:}}
\textit{In Section 3.1, provided a more thorough explanation of the Euler method, including its advantages and disadvantages}
\\
\\
5. In Section 3, implementing a large constant modular multiplication introduces
computational overhead. I suggest optimizing the multiplier block logic and providing a
detailed analysis of its practical feasibility and delay costs on the FPGA.
\\
\\
\textbf{\textit{Response to this suggestion:}}
\textit{Section 3.4 added to explain the selection for the various paramters}
\\
\\
6. In Section 3, the perturbation mechanism modifies chaotic parameters dynamically.
Please provide a rigorous mathematical derivation explaining how these specific
parameter values guarantee stable global chaotic behavior without collapsing into
periodic windows during continuous disruption.
\\
\\
\textbf{\textit{Response to this suggestion:}}
\textit{Section 3.4 to further discuss the selection of parameters and perturbations}
\\
\\
7. In Section 4, the hardware evaluation omits operating frequency data in the FPGA
results. Please report the maximum clock frequency and timing analysis to allow an
independent verification of your hardware performance claims.
\\
\\
\textbf{\textit{Response to this suggestion:}}
\textit{Section 5.3 added to show the timing analysis}
\\
\\
8. In Section 4, the initial key integration relies on static fractional scaling. To prevent
linear relationships between key bits and initial states, I recommend introducing a robust,
nonlinear key scheduling algorithm to enhance cryptographic security.
\\
\\
\textbf{\textit{Response to this suggestion:}}
\textit{In section 6, added the recommendation for future work}
\\
\\
9.  In Section 5, the resource consumption table lists component counts but excludes
latency metrics. Please calculate and present the critical path delay to demonstrate the
true hardware efficiency and practical engineering depth of your design.
\\
\\
\textbf{\textit{Response to this suggestion:}}
\textit{Section 5.3 added to show the timing analysis}
\\
\\
10. In Section 5, the key sensitivity analysis evaluates only a single pair of test keys.
Please compute the average Hamming distance across more random key pairs to establish
a statistically meaningful cryptographic evaluation
\\
\\
\textbf{\textit{Response to this suggestion:}}
\textit{Section 5.4 has been updated to address this concern using 100 keys}
\\
\\
11. In Section 5, the uniformity histogram utilizes a relatively small sample size. To rule
out periodic biases and establish long term statistical uniformity, please significantly
increase the dataset volume to more generated iterations.
\\
\\
\textbf{\textit{Response to this suggestion:}}
\textit{Section 5.5 has been updated, the dataset has been increased with 25.6 Million iterations}
\\
\\
12. In Section 5, calculating the largest Lyapunov exponent directly from the discrete
generator output stream is theoretically problematic. I advise computing this metric
exclusively from the continuous phase space trajectory to reflect the true dynamics.
\\
\\
\textbf{\textit{Response to this suggestion:}}
\textit{Section 3.2 has been updated with the Lyapunov exponent of the enhanced system}
\\
\\
13. Please consider updating the reference list to include more recent studies on chaotic
systems and their diverse applications. This will help highlight the comprehensiveness,
novelty, and superiority of your work.
\\
\\
\textbf{\textit{Response to this suggestion:}}
\textit{Recent studies and research have added to section 2}
\\
